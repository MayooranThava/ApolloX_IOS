Void Runner Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 30, 2026 · Bundle ID com.mayooran.ApolloX

Void Runner (also branded ApolloX in development) is a single-player iOS game. This policy explains what data is used and why.

Data we store on your device

High scores, credit wallet, hangar unlocks, and settings (sound / haptics / onboarding) are saved locally with Apple’s UserDefaults / app storage. This data stays on your device unless you clear the app.

Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple may process your Game Center nickname / player ID and submitted high scores so global leaderboards can work. That data is handled by Apple under their Game Center terms. You can stay signed out; the game still works with a local high score only.

What we do not do

  • No advertising SDKs in the current version
  • No third-party analytics SDKs in the current version
  • No sale of personal data
  • No tracking across apps or websites for advertising

Children

The game does not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what Apple Game Center may process if a player chooses to sign in.

Contact

Questions: use the in-app Settings → Support page or email the developer listed on the App Store product page.
