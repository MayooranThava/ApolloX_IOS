Void Runner Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026 · Bundle ID com.mayooran.ApolloX

Void Runner is a single-player iOS arcade shooter. The developer does not collect data from this app. That matches the App Store privacy label: Data Not Collected.

Data stored only on your device

High scores, credit wallet, hangar unlocks, and settings (sound, music, haptics, onboarding) are saved locally with Apple’s UserDefaults. This data stays on your device. Deleting the app removes it. The developer does not receive it.

Optional Game Center

If you sign in to Game Center, Apple (not the Void Runner developer) processes your Game Center nickname, player ID, and submitted scores so leaderboards and achievements can work. That is covered by Game Center & Privacy. You can stay signed out; the game still works with a local high score only.

What we do not do

  • No developer-operated servers or accounts
  • No advertising SDKs
  • No third-party analytics SDKs
  • No sale of personal data
  • No tracking across apps or websites for advertising
  • No App Tracking Transparency / advertising identifier use

Children

The game is a cartoon space shooter. It does not collect personal information from children. There is no chat, social feed, or in-app purchase.

Your choices

  • Stay signed out of Game Center to keep scores on-device only.
  • Sign out of Game Center in iOS Settings at any time.
  • Delete the app to clear local high scores, credits, and settings.

Contact

Questions: use in-app Settings → Support, or email the developer address listed on the App Store product page for Void Runner.
