Void Runner Support

Portrait arcade shooter for iPhone · com.mayooran.ApolloX

Quick help

  • Sound / haptics: Title → Settings
  • How to play: Settings → How to Play (or the title-screen How to Play button)
  • Ranks: Sign in to Game Center (iOS Settings → Game Center), then open Ranks in the game
  • Ships and weapons: Earn credits from runs, then open Store (Hangar)
  • Pause: Tap pause during a run; the game stays paused if you leave the app
